A NOTE BEFORE THE MAGIC

How to Play

A simple guide to the website experience.

1. Choose your experience

Digital Christmas is played through the website. Physical Collection uses the website alongside the physical boards and cards.

2. Create or join a room

A host creates a room and shares its code. Other players join with that code.

3. Gather around

Use one shared screen, individual phones, or a combination, depending on the challenge.

4. Play the challenges

Each game will have its own objective, rules, components and balanced Grinch interaction.

5. Discover the secret

The hidden-role finale brings the collection together. Its final rules are still being designed.
